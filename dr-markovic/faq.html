Česta pitanjaFrequently asked questions

Odgovori na ono što pacijenti najčešće pitaju pre prve posete.Answers to what patients most often ask before their first visit.

Radno vreme i hitni slučajeviOpening hours and emergencies

Da li radite nedeljom i uveče?Are you open on Sundays and in the evening?

Da. Ordinacija radi svaki dan od 08:00 do 23:30, uključujući vikende.Yes. The clinic is open every day from 8:00 AM to 11:30 PM, including weekends.

Da li primate hitne slučajeve bez zakazivanja?Do you take emergencies without an appointment?

Da. Za jak bol, otok i povrede zuba pozovite nas odmah i dogovorićemo dolazak što pre.Yes. For severe pain, swelling or tooth injuries call us right away and we'll arrange a visit as soon as possible.

Šta ako mi zub ispadne?What if my tooth is knocked out?

Uhvatite ga za krunu, ne dirajte koren, čuvajte ga u mleku i odmah nas pozovite. Što pre stignete, to su šanse bolje.Hold it by the crown, don't touch the root, keep it in milk and call us immediately. The sooner you arrive, the better the chances.

Pacijenti iz inostranstvaVisitors from abroad

Govorite li engleski?Do you speak English?

Da, pacijenti iz inostranstva su dobrodošli i komunikacija na engleskom nije problem.Yes, patients from abroad are welcome and communicating in English is not a problem.

Mogu li doći kao turista, bez ikakvog zakazivanja?Can I come as a tourist without an appointment?

Za hitne slučajeve da, preporučujemo da prvo pozovete. Za ostale zahvate bolje je zakazati termin.For emergencies yes, we recommend calling first. For other procedures it's better to book an appointment.

Lečenje i ceneTreatment and prices

Da li je vađenje zuba bolno?Is tooth extraction painful?

Zahvat radimo uz anesteziju, pa tokom njega ne bi trebalo da osećate bol. Posle dobijate uputstvo za negu.We perform it under anesthesia, so you shouldn't feel pain during the procedure. Afterwards you get aftercare instructions.

Da li pokušavate da sačuvate zub pre vađenja?Do you try to save the tooth before extracting it?

Da. Vađenje je poslednja opcija, a prvo procenjujemo da li zub može da se sanira.Yes. Extraction is the last option, and we first assess whether the tooth can be restored.

Koliko košta pregled i lečenje?How much do exams and treatments cost?

Cena zavisi od zahvata. Posle pregleda dobijate jasnu ponudu pre početka lečenja. The price depends on the procedure. After the exam you get a clear quote before treatment begins.

Kako mogu da platim?How can I pay?

Načine plaćanja potvrdite pri zakazivanju. Please confirm payment methods when booking.
