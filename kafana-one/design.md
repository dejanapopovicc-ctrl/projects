# Kafana Stara Kuća one-page - brief
Type: restaurant/cafe. Action: call to come by. No form. Call + map only.
Refs: planplus/Google listings for the street (Stara Srbija etc.) are bare entries; category default = brown "tradicija" with ornament and stock grill photos; best practice = hours and dishes up front, big warm photos, phone. Borrow: hours board, dish photos. Avoid: ornamental folk borders, "autentično".
Idea: a chalkboard kafana menu - hours and three dishes written like on the board by the door.
Palette: bottle green #1f3a2e, brass #c58b3a, cream #f4ecdc, brick #8c2f1f, ink #1c1a17. Cream on green 10:1, ink on cream 14:1.
Fonts: Playfair Display 700/italic + Karla 400/600.
Hero: centered type with three tilted photo cards. Nav: transparent over hero, solid on scroll, links right. Cards: image-top. Signature: chalkboard hours with live open/closed line.
Differs from last three (cream/terracotta, yellow/black, plum): hero, nav, palette, fonts, cards, signature = 6/7.
