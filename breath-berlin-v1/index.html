Slower breath. Quieter body. More room to think.

I work with people who are tired in a way sleep does not fix. Tight shoulders, a jaw that never unclenches, a mind that keeps running at night.

In a session we start with your breath and your posture. We notice what your body is already doing, and we give it another option. Nothing dramatic. Most people say they feel heavier and lighter at the same time.

You stay clothed. You can speak as much or as little as you like.

What an hour with me looks like

Nothing is a surprise. You know each step before it happens.

First 10 minutes

We talk

What brought you here and what you would like to change. This is also where health questions go. You decide how much to say.

Minutes 10 to 20

You settle

You choose how you sit or lie. We begin with your breath and change nothing yet. We only notice.

Minutes 20 to 50

Slow, guided work

Breath and body awareness, at your pace. You can open your eyes, speak or stop at any point.

Last 10 minutes

You land

We come back slowly. A minute of quiet, then one simple thing to try at home.

I am Mara.

I came to this work after years at a desk and a body that would not switch off. I trained in breathwork and somatic approaches, and I have been seeing clients in Berlin since 2017.

My sessions are quiet and unhurried. I would rather do less, and do it slowly, than fill the hour.

Breathwork and somatic work are complementary support. They do not replace psychotherapy or medical care.

Before you book

What happens in a first session?

We talk for about ten minutes about what you want to change. Then we start with your breath and the way you sit or lie. You stay clothed and you can stop at any point.

Do I need any experience?

No. Most people have never done breathwork. If you can lie on a mat for an hour, you can do this.

Is this therapy?

It is body-based support. It works well next to psychotherapy and does not replace it, or medical care. If you are in treatment, tell your doctor or therapist you are coming.

Who sees what I share?

Only me. I keep notes for my own use and I do not share them. Your email is used to confirm sessions and nothing else.

What if I need to cancel?

Tell me at least 24 hours before and we will move it. Later than that, the session is charged.

Which languages do you work in?

English and German. Say which you prefer when you book.
