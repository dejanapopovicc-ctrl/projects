PregledOverview

Kada se nosi fiksni aparatWhen to wear a fixed brace

Fiksni aparat je glavno sredstvo za ispravljanje položaja zuba i vilica. Providne folije su za odrasle koji ne žele fiksni aparat, a pokretni aparati za malu decu.A fixed brace is the main tool for correcting the position of teeth and jaws. Clear aligners are for adults who do not want a fixed brace, and removable appliances are for young children.

  • Zbijeni, razmaknuti i rotirani zubi, otvoren, duboki i ukršteni zagrižajCrowded, spaced and rotated teeth; open, deep and crossbite
  • Lakše održavanje higijene uz konac i interdentalne četkiceEasier hygiene with floss and interdental brushes
  • Zubi ostaju u ispravljenom položajuTeeth tend to stay in the corrected position
  • Mobilni aparat za decu: 300 €Removable appliance for children: 300 €

Česta pitanjaFAQ

Pitanja o aparatimaBrace questions

Ima li gornje starosne granice?Is there an upper age limit?

Ne. Lečeni su i pacijenti stariji od 50 godina. Najbolje je početi pre 18. godine, kada se zubi brže pomeraju, ali i uzrast 18 do 30 daje dobre rezultate. Lečenje je moguće sve dok postoje prirodni zubi.No. Patients over 50 have been treated. It is best to start before 18 when teeth move faster, but ages 18 to 30 also work well. Treatment is possible as long as you have your own natural teeth.

Koliko traje lečenje?How long does treatment take?

Najmanje 18 meseci, često produženo na 24. Mnogi pacijenti prestanu da primećuju aparat posle 9 do 12 meseci. Kontrole su mesečne, a kod samoligirajućih bravica ponekad ređe.At least 18 months, often extended to 24. Many patients stop noticing the brace after 9 to 12 months. Check-ups are monthly, sometimes less frequent with self-ligating braces.

Šta posle skidanja aparata?What after the brace comes off?

Noću se nosi providna retenciona folija onoliko dugo koliko je nošen aparat. Pacijenti stariji od 30 godina nose je najmanje 2 do 3 puta nedeljno nekoliko godina. Prva retenciona folija je besplatna.A clear retainer is worn at night for as long as the brace was worn. Patients over 30 wear it at least 2 to 3 times a week for a few years. The first retainer is free.

Mora li se lečiti obe vilice?Must both jaws be treated?

Preporučuje se, ali nije uvek obavezno. Obaveze i posete su iste za jednu ili dve vilice, a pacijenti koji leče samo jednu kasnije često trebaju i drugu.It is recommended but not always required. Obligations and visits are the same for one or two jaws, and patients who treat only one often need the other later.

Pošaljite snimak i dobijte plan lečenjaSend your X-ray and get a treatment plan

Online pregled je besplatan. Odgovor stiže uglavnom u roku od oko 48 sati, sa opcijama lečenja i planom po fazama.The online exam is free. A reply usually arrives within about 48 hours, with treatment options and a plan by phase.
