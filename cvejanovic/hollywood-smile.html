PregledOverview

Šta je Hollywood smileWhat a Hollywood smile is

Prirodni zubi se zadržavaju, a prebojeni ili okrnjeni gleđ se obrusi i prekrije keramikom u nijansi koju pacijent izabere. Krunice vraćaju pun kontakt sa desnima, koje se s godinama povlače.Natural teeth are kept; discoloured or chipped enamel is ground down and covered with ceramic in the shade the patient chooses. Crowns restore full contact with the gums, which recede with age.

  • Bez osetljivosti zuba i neprijatnog zadahaNo tooth sensitivity and no bad breath
  • Bolji govor i žvakanje bez ograničenjaBetter speech and unrestricted eating

Tri pristupaThree approaches

Fasete, krunice ili implantiVeneers, crowns or implants

FaseteVeneers

Najmanje invazivno: tanke ljuske na prednjoj strani zdravih zuba. Svi bočni zubi moraju biti prisutni. Samo oko 5% Hollywood smile radova ordinacije je isključivo na fasetama, jer traže visok nivo očuvanosti zuba.The least invasive: thin shells on the front of healthy teeth. All side teeth must be present. Only about 5% of the clinic's Hollywood smiles are veneers only, because they demand a high level of tooth preservation.

KruniceCrowns

Najčešći izbor. Pokrivaju ceo zub, koriste se kada zubi ili njihovi delovi nedostaju ili im oblik, položaj i boja nisu zadovoljavajući, i omogućavaju mostove. Prva popravka je obično potrebna tek posle najmanje 5 do 7 godina.The most common choice. They cover the whole tooth, are used when teeth or parts of them are missing or their shape, position and colour are unsatisfactory, and allow bridges. The first repair is usually needed only after at least 5 to 7 years.

ImplantiImplants

Zamenjuju zube koji nedostaju u okviru plana Hollywood smile. Ponekad su potrebna do četiri implanta u bočnim regionima, što blago povećava cenu.They replace missing teeth within the Hollywood smile plan. Sometimes up to four implants are needed in the side regions, which slightly increases the cost.

Rizici i garancijaRisks and warranty

Nema oralne hirurgije, pa se ozbiljne komplikacije tokom zahvata smatraju malo verovatnim. Najčešće su upala posle zahvata ili pucanje krunice godinama kasnije. Garancija je 5 godina i pokriva navedene komplikacije.There is no oral surgery, so serious complications during the procedure are considered unlikely. The most common are inflammation afterwards or a crown cracking years later. The warranty is 5 years and covers these complications.

Česta pitanjaFAQ

Pitanja o Hollywood smileHollywood smile questions

Da li je parodontopatija prepreka?Is periodontitis a barrier?

Ne obavezno. Parodontopatija u ranoj fazi može se zaustaviti pre nego što se zubi olabave. Kada krunice dobro naležu, bolest se obično zaustavlja jer bakterije ne dolaze do desni. Lečenje uključuje mehaničko čišćenje, antibiotike i laser i naplaćuje se dodatno.Not necessarily. Early-stage periodontitis can be halted before teeth loosen. Once crowns fit well, the disease usually stops because bacteria cannot reach the gums. Treatment uses mechanical cleaning, antibiotics and laser and is charged extra.

Zašto se više ne rade metalokeramičke krunice?Why are metal-ceramic crowns no longer done?

Ordinacija ih ne nudi otprilike od 2020. godine. Koštale su oko 100 €, upola manje od punih keramičkih. Kod otprilike polovine pacijenata dolazilo je do postepenog povlačenja desni jer metal nije u potpunosti biokompatibilan, metal se mogao videti na rubu desni, a krunice su pod nekim svetlom delovale sivkasto.The clinic has not offered them since around 2020. They cost about 100 €, half the price of full ceramic ones. About half of patients had gradual gum recession because the metal was not fully biocompatible, metal could show at the gumline and crowns could look greyish in some light.

Za koga nije preporučen Hollywood smile?Who is Hollywood smile not recommended for?

Ispod 30 godina, naročito ako se problem može rešiti blažim metodama: protezom, beljenjem ili fasetama. Izuzetak su zanimanja koja traže savršene zube.Under 30, especially if the problem can be solved by milder methods: a denture, whitening or veneers. The exception is professions that require perfect teeth.
