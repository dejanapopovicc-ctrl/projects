KonceptThe concept

Rešenje za potpunu bezubostA solution for full toothlessness

ALL ON 4 i ALL ON 6 koriste se kada su preostali zubi pred ispadanjem ili ih više nema. Cela vilica dobija fiksne zube na četiri ili šest implanata. Dr Srđan Cvejanović je prema podacima ordinacije jedan od vodećih stručnjaka za ovaj koncept u regionu, a ordinacija ga primenjuje više od deset godina.ALL ON 4 and ALL ON 6 are used when the remaining teeth are about to fall out or are already gone. The whole jaw receives fixed teeth on four or six implants. According to the clinic, Dr Srđan Cvejanović is among the leading experts in this concept in the region, and the clinic has applied it for more than ten years.

Tok lečenjaCourse of treatment

Šest faza ALL ON 6 radaSix phases of an ALL ON 6 restoration

Pacijenti iz inostranstva dolaze dvaput, u razmaku od oko tri meseca.Patients from abroad visit twice, about three months apart.

01

PlaniranjePlanning

Pregled, snimak i plan. Idealno je 3D CBCT snimanje, a ako ga nemate u svojoj zemlji, radi se po dolasku u Srbiju.Examination, X-ray and plan. A 3D CBCT scan is ideal, and if you cannot get one at home it is done on arrival in Serbia.

02

OperacijaSurgery

Implanti obe vilice mogu da se ugrade u jednom danu, najčešće na mestima gde su bili 2., 4. i 6. zub. Operacija je vođena računarom, a sedacija je dostupna.The implants of both jaws can be placed in a single day, mostly where the 2nd, 4th and 6th teeth used to be. Surgery is computer-guided and sedation is available.

03

Privremeni zubiTemporary teeth

Pacijent odlazi sa fiksnim privremenim zubima, ušrafljenim na implante. Nose se tokom perioda srastanja.The patient leaves with fixed temporary teeth screwed onto the implants, worn during the integration period.

04

Srastanje kostiBone integration

Traje između 3 i 6 meseci. Pacijent iz inostranstva ovo vreme provodi kod kuće.It lasts between 3 and 6 months. A patient from abroad spends this time at home.

05

Definitivni radFinal restoration

Titanijumska konstrukcija izrađena 3D štampom, šuplja i laka, sa keramikom spolja. Ušrafljuje se na implante, bez cementiranja.A 3D-printed titanium framework, hollow and light, with ceramic on the outside. It is screwed onto the implants, with no cement.

06

KontroleCheck-ups

Redovne kontrole svakih šest meseci održavaju doživotnu garanciju na implante.Regular check-ups every six months keep the lifetime implant warranty valid.

Zubi za 3 sataTeeth in 3 hours

Budite se sa zubimaWake up with teeth

Zubi za 3 sata je naziv koji ordinacija koristi za momentalno opterećenje implanata: privremeni rad se postavlja na tek ugrađene implante, pa se pacijent budi iz anestezije sa već postavljenim zubima.Teeth in 3 hours is the clinic's name for immediate loading of implants: a temporary restoration is fitted on freshly placed implants, so the patient wakes from anaesthesia with teeth already in place.

  • Titanijum i keramika za definitivni radTitanium and ceramic for the final work
  • Alternativa: proteza na drikerima, funkcionalna ali na nižem nivouAlternative: a denture on attachments, functional but at a lower level
Česta pitanjaFAQ

Šta pacijenti pitajuWhat patients ask

Čime se ALL ON 6 razlikuje od ALL ON 4?How does ALL ON 6 differ from ALL ON 4?

Spolja izgledaju isto, ali ALL ON 6 stoji na šest implanata umesto na četiri, pa je pouzdaniji i lakši za popravku. ALL ON 4 se bira kada je kost slabija.From the outside they look identical, but ALL ON 6 sits on six implants instead of four, so it is more reliable and easier to repair. ALL ON 4 is chosen when the bone is weaker.

Šta ako je kost slaba?What if the bone is weak?

Pri ALL ON 4 i ALL ON 6 zahvatima rešavaju se i najteži slučajevi nedostatka kosti. Prednost ovog koncepta je što u mnogim slučajevima nije potrebna dodatna nadoknada kosti.ALL ON 4 and ALL ON 6 procedures can resolve even severe bone deficiency. A benefit of the concept is that in many cases no extra bone grafting is needed.

Šta su privremeni zubi?What are the temporary teeth?

To nisu odvojene krunice, već plastična konstrukcija u kojoj su svi zubi jedne vilice spojeni i ušrafljeni na implante. Lako se postavljaju i skidaju, a služe da se proveri izgled budućeg definitivnog rada.They are not separate crowns but a plastic structure in which all teeth of one jaw are joined and screwed onto the implants. They are easy to fit and remove and let the dentist check how the final work will look.

Koliko je komplikacija?How common are complications?

Rad je veoma trajan i najčešće traje doživotno. Komplikacije se javljaju kod oko 3% slučajeva, gotovo uvek pre postavljanja definitivnog rada, a šansa da se implanti ne integrišu u kost je oko 1%. U tom slučaju pacijent je pokriven garancijom.The work is very durable and usually lasts a lifetime. Complications occur in about 3% of cases, almost always before the final restoration, and the chance that implants fail to integrate with the bone is about 1%. In that case the patient is covered by the warranty.

Kako se održava higijena?How is hygiene maintained?

Higijena oko implanata je ključna da bi se izbegao peri-implantitis, upala oko implanata. Pušenje je jedan od važnih faktora rizika, a ordinacija pacijentima pokazuje kako da čiste ALL ON 6 rad.Hygiene around implants is key to avoiding peri-implantitis, inflammation around implants. Smoking is an important risk factor, and the clinic shows patients how to clean an ALL ON 6 restoration.
