Zašto implantWhy an implant

Šest razloga za implantSix reasons for an implant

01

Zamenjuje koren zubaReplaces the tooth root

Implant sprečava gubitak kosti koji sledi nakon gubitka zuba.An implant prevents the bone loss that follows tooth loss.

02

Čuva susedne zubeProtects neighbouring teeth

Bez implanta se okolni zubi pomeraju i zagrižaj postaje neravnomeran.Without an implant, nearby teeth drift and the bite becomes uneven.

03

Bioaktivna integracijaBioactive integration

Moderni implanti su takvi da kost raste oko njih i oni postaju deo organizma.Modern implants are designed so that bone grows around them and they become part of the body.

04

Bez brušenja zdravih zubaNo grinding of healthy teeth

Most na implantima ne zahteva brušenje susednih zuba, a opterećenje prenosi direktno na kost.An implant bridge does not need grinding of neighbouring teeth, and it transfers load directly to the bone.

05

Izgled i samopouzdanjeLooks and confidence

Vraća izgled i samopouzdanje u svakom dobu.Restores appearance and confidence at any age.

06

Što ranije, to boljeThe earlier, the better

Ugradnja ubrzo nakon gubitka zuba daje bolje rezultate.Placing an implant soon after tooth loss gives better results.

Kada se koristiWhen it is used

Tri najčešće situacijeThe three most common situations

Jedan zub koji nedostajeOne missing tooth

Idealan slučaj: kost je jaka, desni se nisu povukle, a susedni zubi pomažu u oblikovanju krunice. Rezultat izgleda prirodno.The ideal case: the bone is strong, the gums have not receded and neighbouring teeth help shape the crown. The result looks natural.

Nekoliko zuba koji nedostajuSeveral missing teeth

Jedan implant za jedan ili dva zuba. Dva implanta mogu da nose most koji zamenjuje tri do četiri zuba.One implant for one or two teeth. Two implants can carry a bridge replacing three to four teeth.

Delimična ili potpuna bezubostPartial or full toothlessness

Kada nedostaje više od 10 zuba ili su zubi nestabilni zbog parodontopatije: ALL ON 6 ili ALL ON 4, sa trajnom titanijumsko-keramičkom konstrukcijom.When more than 10 teeth are missing or teeth are loose from periodontitis: ALL ON 6 or ALL ON 4 with a permanent titanium-ceramic framework.

Snimak pokazuje da li kost ima dovoljno zapremine. Zavisno od slučaja, pacijent može prvo dobiti privremenu plastičnu krunicu, a keramičku kasnije, ili se keramička kruna glodaće na licu mesta CEREC aparatom i odmah postaviti.A scan shows whether the bone has enough volume. Depending on the case, the patient may first receive a temporary plastic crown with the ceramic one later, or the ceramic crown can be milled on site with the CEREC machine and placed at once.

Česta pitanjaFAQ

Pitanja o implantimaQuestions about implants

Da li su implanti za starije pacijente?Are implants suitable for older patients?

Većina pacijenata u sedamdesetim godinama može dobiti implante, a pacijenti u šezdesetim gotovo sigurno. Snimak pokazuje stanje kosti, a prosečno stanje je dovoljno. Ordinacija smatra da stariji pacijenti često nepotrebno nose pokretne proteze.Most patients in their seventies can receive implants, and patients in their sixties almost certainly can. A scan shows the condition of the bone, and an average condition is enough. The clinic notes that older patients often wear removable dentures unnecessarily.

Kada postaje kasno za implant?When does it become too late?

U roku od nekoliko meseci susedni zubi ostaju na mestu i kruna dobro popunjava prazninu. Posle oko godinu dana zubi se mogu pomeriti ili se kost povući, pa su potrebni dodatna priprema i prilagođena kruna. Posle nekoliko godina implant je i dalje opcija, ali je složeniji i često traži augmentaciju kosti ili ortodontsko lečenje.Within a few months neighbouring teeth stay in place and the crown fills the gap well. After about a year teeth may shift or bone may recede, so extra preparation and an adjusted crown may be needed. After several years an implant is still an option but more complex, often needing bone augmentation or orthodontic treatment.

Zašto većina zubara ne radi ALL ON 4?Why do most dentists not offer ALL ON 4?

Tehnički je zahtevniji od ALL ON 6, traži veliko implantološko iskustvo i nosi rizik korekcija u garantnom roku. Ordinacija navodi da je ALL ON 4 radila kod mnogo pacijenata bez problema.It is technically more demanding than ALL ON 6, needs substantial implantology experience and carries the risk of corrections during the warranty period. The clinic says it has done ALL ON 4 for many patients without problems.

Može li novi implant tamo gde je bio stari?Can a new implant go where an old one was?

Obično da. Kost mora da zaceli posle uklanjanja starog implanta, a može biti potrebna augmentacija. Ako implant otkaže, obično se ukloni, prostor se regeneriše koštanim materijalom, a novi implant se ugrađuje nakon nekoliko meseci.Usually yes. The bone must heal after the old implant is removed and augmentation may be needed. If an implant fails it is typically removed, the space regenerated with bone material and a new implant placed after several months.

Most na implantima ili na prirodnim zubima?Implant bridge or natural-tooth bridge?

Most na implantima ne zahteva brušenje zdravih susednih zuba. Implanti prenose silu direktno na kost i sprečavaju njen gubitak, dok kod klasičnog mosta kost ispod praznine propada.An implant bridge does not require grinding healthy neighbouring teeth. Implants transfer force directly to bone and prevent its loss, whereas under a conventional bridge the bone below the gap degrades.
