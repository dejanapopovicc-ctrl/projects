Zašto kruniceWhy crowns

Četiri razloga zbog kojih pacijenti dolazeFour reasons patients come

Ordinacija navodi da krunice rešavaju većinu stomatoloških problema, a danas većina pacijenata dolazi zbog izgleda.The clinic says crowns solve most dental problems, and today most patients come for appearance.

01

Požutelo zubiYellowed teeth

Zubi su pravilni i jaki, ali su s godinama požuteli, pa beljenje ne bi mnogo pomoglo.Teeth are straight and strong but have yellowed with age, so whitening would not help much.

Oblik i položaj su prihvatljivi, ali zubi deluju žućkasto, sitno ili razmaknuto.Shape and position are acceptable, but teeth look yellowish, small or spaced.

03

04

Zubi koji nedostajuMissing teeth

Jedan ili više zuba nedostaje, sa ili bez ostalih problema.One or more teeth are missing, with or without other issues.

Sa implantom ili bezWith or without an implant

Kada je implant potrebanWhen an implant is needed

Ako je koren zdrav, nema uznapredovale parodontopatije i ima dovoljno zuba, ordinacija uvek bira krunicu bez implanta. Takva je jeftinija, brža, manje bolna i prirodno se uklapa u desni. Za jedan ili dva susedna zuba koji nedostaju krunicu može da nosi implant. Kruna izgleda isto, a razlika je na liniji desni: zdrave desni daju prirodan prelaz, a povučene traže ružičastu keramiku.If the root is healthy, there is no advanced periodontitis and enough tooth remains, the clinic always chooses a crown without an implant. It is cheaper, quicker, less painful and blends naturally into the gums. For one or two adjacent missing teeth an implant can carry the crown. The crown looks the same; the difference is at the gumline: healthy gums give a natural transition, receded gums need pink ceramic.

Kada krunica stoji pored prirodnih zuba, oni se obično prvo izbele.Single crowns match neighbouring teeth. In a full Hollywood smile the patient chooses: Bleach 1 is the brightest, Bleach 2 the most common and Bleach 4 the most natural. When a crown sits beside natural teeth, they are usually whitened first.

Česta pitanjaFAQ

Pitanja o krunicamaCrown questions

Koliko traju krunice?How long do crowns last?

Obično 15 godina i duže, često i doživotno. Vek zavisi od opterećenja i sila zagrižaja, ishrane, stiskanja i škrgutanja, stanja zuba i korena ispod, kao i od higijene. Dobra higijena, redovne kontrole i uravnotežen zagrižaj čuvaju rezultat.Usually 15 years or more, often for life. Lifespan depends on load and bite forces, diet, clenching or grinding, the condition of the tooth and root underneath, and hygiene. Good hygiene, regular check-ups and a balanced bite keep the result.

Hoću li primetiti razliku?Will I notice a difference?

Na početku je normalno, jer je kruna strano telo u ustima. Osećaj je blag i traje nekoliko dana. Krunice u Hollywood smile radu prate prirodne kontakte i oblik zuba, pa je osećaj gotovo identičan prirodnim zubima.At first this is normal, as the crown is a foreign object in the mouth. The sensation is mild and lasts a few days. Crowns in a Hollywood smile follow natural contacts and tooth shape, so the feel is nearly identical to natural teeth.

Da li je Hollywood smile za mlađe?Is Hollywood smile for younger patients?

Ne preporučuje se ispod 30 godina, naročito ako se estetski problem rešava jednostavnijim metodama: protezom, beljenjem i fasetama. Preko 30 godina izbor je slobodan, uz razgovor o alternativama. Cilj ordinacije je da prirodni zubi ostanu dok su zdravi i estetski prihvatljivi.It is not recommended under 30, especially if the aesthetic problem can be solved by simpler methods: a brace, whitening and veneers. Over 30 the choice is free, with alternatives discussed. The clinic's goal is to keep natural teeth as long as they are healthy and aesthetically acceptable.
