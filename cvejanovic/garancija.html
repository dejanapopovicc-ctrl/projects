GarancijaWarranty

Implanti: doživotna garancijaImplants: lifetime warranty

  • Izgubljen implant bez nadoknade: ordinacija ga zamenjuje o svom trošku, ako kost kvantitetom i kvalitetom to dozvoljava.Implant lost with no prosthesis: the clinic replaces it at its own cost, if bone quality and quantity allow.
  • Izgubljen implant sa nadoknadom u garantnom roku, uz redovne kontrole: zamenjuju se i implant i nadoknada, ako to dozvoljavaju kost i dimenzije.Implant lost with a prosthesis under warranty, with regular check-ups: both the implant and the prosthesis are replaced, if bone and dimensions allow.
  • Izgubljen implant bez nadoknade, više od 5 godina posle ugradnje: novi implant o trošku ordinacije, a pacijent plaća 50% redovne cene protetskog dela.Implant lost with no prosthesis, more than 5 years after placement: a new implant at the clinic's cost, and the patient pays 50% of the regular price of the prosthetic part.
  • Ako novi implant nije moguć, radi se augmentacija kosti ili se menja protetski plan.If a new implant is not possible, bone augmentation is done or the prosthetic plan is changed.

Krunice, fasete, mostovi: 5 godinaCrowns, veneers, bridges: 5 years

  • Besplatna zamena novim protetskim radom nakon većeg okrnjenja keramike ili loma konstrukcije mosta.Free replacement with new prosthetic work after major ceramic chipping or a fractured bridge structure.
  • Promena boje ili oblika posle probe nije pokrivena; izmene su moguće samo do probe.Changes of colour or shape after the try-in are not covered; changes can only be made up to that fitting.
Uslovi:Conditions: kontrola na svakih šest meseci i panoramski snimak jednom godišnje. Garancija ne pokriva fizička oštećenja, kao ni troškove prevoza, smeštaja i druge lične troškove.check-ups every six months and a panoramic X-ray once a year. The warranty does not cover physical damage or transport, accommodation and other private costs.
