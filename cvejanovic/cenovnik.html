Cene su informativne i preuzete sa sajta ordinacije; konačnu cenu potvrđuje ordinacija. Sve cene su u evrima, a obračun se radi po prodajnom kursu Raiffeisen Banke na dan plaćanja. Plaćanje na rate povećava cenu ortodontskog aparata za 100 €. Online pregled je besplatan.Prices are indicative and taken from the clinic's website; the clinic confirms the final price. All prices are in euros, converted at the Raiffeisen Bank selling rate on the day of payment. Paying in instalments adds 100 € to the price of an orthodontic appliance. The online exam is free.
