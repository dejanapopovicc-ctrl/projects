Česta pitanjaFrequently asked questions

Da li je online pregled besplatan?Is the online exam free?

Da, besplatan je. Pregled u ordinaciji se naplaćuje. Plan lečenja obično stiže za oko 48 sati.Yes, it is free. The in-person exam is charged. A treatment plan usually arrives within about 48 hours.

Koliko se čeka na termin?How long is the wait for an appointment?

Pacijent koji je prošao online pregled može odmah da dobije termin, a kasniji termin je moguć po želji.A patient who has completed the online exam can get an appointment right away, and a later one is possible on request.

Koliko puta moram da dođem iz inostranstva?How many times must I come from abroad?

Hollywood smile se završava za manje od 14 dana uz jedno putovanje. ALL ON 6 traži dve posete u razmaku od oko tri meseca.A Hollywood smile is completed in under 14 days with one trip. ALL ON 6 needs two visits about three months apart.

Koliko košta jedan zub na implantu?How much is one tooth on an implant?

Implant, abatment i bezmetalna krunica koštaju ukupno 670 €.An implant, abutment and metal-free crown cost 670 € in total.

Da li postoji garancija?Is there a warranty?

Doživotna na implante i 5 godina na krunice, fasete i mostove, uz kontrole na svakih šest meseci i godišnji panoramski snimak.Lifetime on implants and 5 years on crowns, veneers and bridges, with check-ups every six months and an annual panoramic X-ray.

Da li je zahvat bolan?Is the procedure painful?

Uvek se koristi lokalna anestezija. Za složenije zahvate postoji oralna i intravenska sedacija, nakon koje se većina pacijenata ne seća zahvata.Local anaesthesia is always used. For complex procedures oral and IV sedation are available, after which most patients do not remember the procedure.

Koliko košta sedacija?How much is sedation?

Intravenska sedacija je 700 € i nudi se za radove vredne preko 2.000 €. Oralna sedacija i regionalna anestezija se ne naplaćuju dodatno.IV sedation is 700 € and is offered for work worth over 2,000 €. Oral sedation and regional anaesthesia carry no extra charge.

Gde se smeštam i kako stižem sa aerodroma?Where do I stay and how do I get from the airport?

Dva apartmana su na spratu iznad ordinacije (50 € po danu, depozit 500 €), a transfer od i do aerodroma je 50 € po pravcu.Two apartments are on the floor above the clinic (50 € per day, 500 € deposit) and the airport transfer is 50 € each way.

Na kojim jezicima mogu da komuniciram?Which languages can I use?

Na engleskom, francuskom, nemačkom i italijanskom, kao i na srpskom.English, French, German and Italian, as well as Serbian.

Kako se plaća i po kom kursu?How is payment made and at what rate?

Cene su u evrima, a obračun se radi po prodajnom kursu Raiffeisen Banke na dan plaćanja. Za ortodontske aparate moguće je plaćanje na rate, uz uvećanje od 100 €.Prices are in euros, converted at the Raiffeisen Bank selling rate on the day of payment. Orthodontic appliances can be paid in instalments, adding 100 €.

Radno vreme?Opening hours?

Ponedeljak do petak, od 08:00 do 16:00.Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00.

Detaljnija pitanja o implantima, krunicama, fasetama, aparatima i sedaciji nalaze se na stranicama tih usluga.More detailed questions on implants, crowns, veneers, braces and sedation are on the pages for those services.

Pošaljite snimak i dobijte plan lečenjaSend your X-ray and get a treatment plan

Online pregled je besplatan. Odgovor stiže uglavnom u roku od oko 48 sati, sa opcijama lečenja i planom po fazama.The online exam is free. A reply usually arrives within about 48 hours, with treatment options and a plan by phase.
