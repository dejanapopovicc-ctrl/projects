FAQ

Good to know

What happens in the first session?

We talk about what brings you here and what you would like to change. There is no pressure to share more than you want to, and you decide whether we continue.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Use the link in your confirmation message to move or cancel your session up to 24 hours before it starts.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both are possible. You choose when you book.
