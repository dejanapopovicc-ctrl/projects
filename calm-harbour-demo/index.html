What we can work on together

You do not need a diagnosis or a crisis to start. Most people come because something feels heavier than it should.

    Quieting the loop and building calmer habits.

  • Burnout and work stress

    Boundaries, rest and a more sustainable pace.

  • Relationships

    Communication, trust and difficult conversations.

  • Life transitions

    Moving, new roles, endings and fresh starts.

  • Low mood and motivation

    Small, realistic steps when everything feels flat.

  • Self-esteem

    Being kinder to yourself and trusting your judgement.

FAQ

Good to know

What happens in the first session?

We talk about what brings you here and what you would like to change. There is no pressure to share more than you want to, and you decide whether we continue.

Is what I say confidential?

Sessions are private. In your first session we explain the few exceptions, such as when someone's safety is at risk.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you want to work on. Many people start weekly or every two weeks and review together after a few sessions.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Use the link in your confirmation message to move or cancel your session up to 24 hours before it starts.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both are possible. You choose when you book.
