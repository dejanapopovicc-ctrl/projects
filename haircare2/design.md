# Hair Care House v2 - design brief

## Research takeaways (searched: salon website roundups, Vracar directory)
- Category default: black/gold or black-white "luxury", full-bleed hero photo, generic "Book now". Belgrade neighbours (Vracar directory) are mostly listings without real sites.
- What the best do: editorial type, named signature ritual, honest "from" price rows, stylist/ritual cards, calm cream and terracotta boutique palettes (Strange Bird, Blonde Faith pattern).
- Strongest proof for this client: 922 reviews at 4.7 and repair after bleaching. So: big numerals + editorial journal.

## Copy brief
Customer: woman 25-50 in Belgrade, doubts: "will bleaching wreck my hair", "will colour match what I imagine". Promise: colour that comes out as imagined, hair that stays healthy. Tone: warm, calm, precise, human. Feel: lived-in, soft, honest. Not: glossy-luxury, loud, clinical.

## Design
- Archetype: printed hair journal / lookbook. Idea: every service is an "issue" with a number.
- Palette: paper #f3ede3, surface #fbf8f2, ink #1f1b18, accent terracotta #b8532f, support sage #7f8a6d, hairline #d9cfc0. Body contrast ink on paper > 14:1, white on terracotta 4.9:1.
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (display, italic emphasis) + Work Sans (body). Never Fraunces.
- Shape: arches (arched photo crops), hairline rules, no shadows, square buttons with underline arrows.
- Hero: editorial two-column, huge numeral 4,7 beside arched portrait.
- Nav: centered wordmark, links row below (two-tier), live open-now chip.
- Cards: numbered list rows. Gallery: horizontal scroll-snap lookbook of arches. Sections: alternating paper/surface, full-bleed terracotta quote band, vertical timeline.
- Signature element: open-now chip (JS from Mon-Sat 09-19, Europe/Belgrade) + oversized review numerals.
- Imagery: warm natural daylight, film grain, cream/terracotta, no faces.

## Differs from v1 (dark plum + rose gold, Manrope, full-bleed hero, boxed cards, bento gallery, single-row nav)
hero, palette, fonts, cards, gallery, nav, signature: 7 of 7.
